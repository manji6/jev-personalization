Get started
Learn how to build a campaign
People interested in moisture-wicking polo shirts
That's it. Your audience is set.

  • Set up decisions in plain words

    Enter what you want to show and the interests or states of your audience. You can review the exact Yes/No questions that will be used.

  • Observe with one shared site tag

    Browsing order, display time and page information are collected automatically. Add states such as applications, bookings or carts only when you need them.

  • Prepare displays that fit your site

    Modal, floating and notification-bar displays with a shared design. Custom JavaScript is supported too.

  • Check safely before delivering

    Drafts, one-time previews on real pages and a diagnostic panel. Environment updates also go from Preview to Production.
